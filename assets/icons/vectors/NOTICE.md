# Coolicons v4.1

These 421 SVG files are unchanged artwork by **Kryston Schwarze**, from
[Coolicons v4.1](https://github.com/krystonschwarze/coolicons/tree/v4.1),
commit `42d70477683a0c44cd1732b32a9def8a94469c5a`.

[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
as stated in the upstream v4.1 README. Retain this attribution when redistributing.

Files are grouped and renamed to match the client screenshot labels; SVG contents
are copied without changes. The importer and `tool/supplemental_vectors.json`
record upstream filenames, the pinned commit, and SHA-256 checksums. Runtime tinting
follows the app theme. Original screenshot crops remain in
`assets/icons/supplemental/` for reference and are excluded from the app bundle.
